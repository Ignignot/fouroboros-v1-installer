Step 1 · Connect & check

Plug the FM-1 into this phone (or computer) with a USB data cable, straight in — no hub. Close any other app or tab that might be using MIDI.

Your FM-1 reports:

—

This FM-1 isn't on the official V15 yet. FOUROBOROS V1 installs from the official V15 (identity FM-1_015) — that's the layout its package is built for. Put the official V15 on first: the Restore section at the bottom of this page does exactly that (it works as the way onto V15 too), or use M-VAVE's M-UPGRADE app on a computer. Then come back and press Connect again.

Step 2 · Install

Writing — keep hands off. Don't unplug the cable, don't power the FM-1 off, and don't close this page until it says Done.

The package check and Step 1 come first.

Done! 🎉 Your FM-1 restarted and reports FM-1_900 — FOUROBOROS V1 is installed. Unplug, open SLOOP's visualiser, pick style 13 · FOUROBOROS, and turn a knob to start. The manual shows the rest.

How it works, in plain words: this page first reads parts of the package with the running firmware and stages a small update loader (nothing is written yet). The FM-1 then restarts into that loader, pulls the whole package and writes it, restarts again, and this page checks it now reports FM-1_900. Your SLOOP projects and settings live outside the firmware area and are not touched.

If anything stalls: replug the cable and press Install again — an FM-1 left in update mode simply finishes the write.

Restore original FM-1 firmware

The calm way back. This installs M-VAVE's official V15 (FM-1_015) — the proven base for FOUROBOROS V1, and the version every tool in this family was tested against.

Heads-up: the official firmware can't open FOUROBOROS/SLOOP projects. If you made music you love, back it up first (SLOOP editor → Projects → Backup) — reinstalling FOUROBOROS brings it back.

About V16 (please read if your FM-1 is newer)

M-VAVE released a stock V16 on 2026-10-21. Newer isn't automatically safer here: nobody in this firmware family has tested installing from V16 or returning to it, and FOUROBOROS V1's package is built for the V15 layout. So this page restores V15 only and will refuse a V16 file with an explanation. (For the record, V16's package is FM-1_016, 718,196 bytes, SHA-256 d017fb65e70aca97ee29d944470c3515a74581df5c0576bf70de917c7b2b61d5 — recorded for future compatibility work, not used.) If your FM-1 already runs V16, talk to us before installing; the M-UPGRADE route to V15 is the careful path.

The V15 recovery image (sdk/firmware/FM-1_015.fwsc) is M-VAVE's own file, distributed by M-VAVE under the Apache License 2.0 (see THIRD_PARTY.md in the fm1_open repository). This page downloads or accepts your copy at the moment of use and redistributes nothing.

Advanced details & log

Update engine: the Felucca/SLOOP WebMIDI updater (M-UPGRADE-compatible protocol over USB-MIDI SysEx; the device pulls the image; the loader never writes the flash head; an interrupted write stays in update mode and resumes).
